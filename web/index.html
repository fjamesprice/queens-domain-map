    Controls

    • Left drag: orbit around the point you're looking at. Wheel: zoom toward the cursor.
    • Right drag: look around from where the camera is.
    • Middle drag or Shift+drag: pan.
    • W A S D fly in the direction you're looking, Q E straight down and up. The Fly speed slider sets the speed; hold Shift or Space for 4× faster (in Firefox, use Space while right-dragging, as Shift + right-click opens the browser menu). While a movement key is held, left drag also looks around, so you can steer with one hand on the keys.
    • Double-click the world to orbit around that spot.
    • Click a marker for details. The URL updates so you can share it.
    • Cut away above slices off everything above a height, which helps with dungeons, towers and caves.
    • Dimmed markers are behind walls or underground. Turn off Markers through walls to hide them.
    • Turn off Textures for flat colors, which are lighter on slower computers.
    • With Enemies, Bosses or Mimics switched on in Filters, a model of each enemy stands at its spawn point in its idle pose. Enemy, NPC, skeleton and wildlife models show within 150 m of the camera, when not hidden behind walls.
    • Areas load as the camera gets within about 100 m of them (areas below you count as further away), so the first view doesn't give away the rest of the game. Use Show all in the Areas tab to load everything at once. The endgame areas start hidden; tick them in the Areas list to see them.

    Pickup labels

    • Hidden: tucked away, often behind a secret wall or in a dark corner.
    • Out of view: off the obvious path, look around corners and ledges.
    • Accessible later: needs a key, ability or route you get later.

    Data is extracted from the game files (build 13540). Positions are exact; some pickups only appear after an event.
